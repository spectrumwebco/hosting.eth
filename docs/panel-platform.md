# Panel Platform

The hosting.eth Panel Platform is a comprehensive solution for managing domains and associated services.

## Key Features

- **Domain Management**: Register and manage both traditional and blockchain domains
- **Service Integration**: Access and manage services from the elest.io library
- **Security**: Keep services tightly coupled to domains without requiring public access
- **Customization**: Custom paneling for open-source projects

## Benefits

- **Reduced Security Risks**: By keeping services behind the Panel Platform, residual cyberthreats are not exposed
- **Simplified Management**: Access all services through a single interface
- **Cost Efficiency**: Leverage cost-friendly alternatives to industry standards
